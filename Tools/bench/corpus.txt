The sync engine keeps a local copy of every record and writes changes to a queue before sending them to the server. If the device is offline, the queue just grows until the connection comes back. Conflicts are resolved by comparing timestamps, and the most recent write wins. This is simple, but it means two people editing the same field at the same time can overwrite each other without any warning.
